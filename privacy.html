Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Anna Creator is run by Yana Sklyar (יאנה סקליאר), a sole proprietor registered in Israel, Menachem 8, Haifa 3313101 (“we”, “us”). We build and operate AI assistants (chatbots) for businesses on WhatsApp, Telegram and other messaging channels. This policy explains what data those assistants and this website handle and how.

Who controls your data

When you write to a business through an assistant we operate, that business is the controller of your conversation data and we process it on its behalf. When you contact us directly, we are the controller.

What we collect

  • Messages you send to the assistant, including text, photos, documents and voice messages, and the replies sent to you.
  • Your messaging identifiers: phone number or WhatsApp ID, Telegram user ID or username, and the profile name you have set.
  • Details you choose to share in the conversation, such as your name, the service you need and the time you want to book.
  • Technical records needed to deliver messages: message IDs, timestamps and delivery status.

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

How we use it

  • To answer your messages and help you on behalf of the business.
  • To book, change or cancel appointments in the business’s calendar and send you reminders.
  • To pass your request, photos and a short summary of the conversation to the business owner.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Service providers

To run the assistants we use: Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform), Telegram, OpenAI (generating replies; data sent through the API is not used to train its models), Google Cloud and Google Calendar (hosting and bookings), Supabase (database) and an email delivery provider. Some of them process data outside Israel, under their own security and data protection terms.

How long we keep it

Conversation data is kept while the business uses our service and for up to 12 months after the last message, unless the business or you ask us to delete it sooner, or the law requires us to keep it longer.

Your rights and data deletion

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time. To delete your data, email yana@annacreator.co.il with the subject “Delete my data” and the phone number or username you wrote from. We will delete your conversations and contact details within 30 days and confirm by email. You can also stop using the assistant at any time by not writing to it or by blocking it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with access limited to the people who need it to run the service.

Changes and contact
